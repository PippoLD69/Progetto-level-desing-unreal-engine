## 2. Descrizione (campo **Description**)

```
KINETIC PROTOCOL RUN
NEOKINETIC // IMPACT RESEARCH DIVISION

A school project in game design and level design, made with Unreal Engine 5 at ITS Academy ICT Piemonte.

You are UNIT 07, a crash-test mannequin dropped onto an experimental test track suspended high above a neon city lost in fog and rain. NEOKINETIC wants impact data. You just want to reach the green checkpoint in one piece.

Sprint, slide, vault and climb through a vertical obstacle course built to break you: swinging pendulum hammers, rotating turnstiles, knockback arms, force fields and a zero-gravity chamber. Every hit throws your body into full physics ragdoll. Get back up, find your line and keep moving.

FEATURES
- Hand-crafted third-person obstacle course in a cyberpunk night setting
- Fluid Motion Matching locomotion: run, slide, vault, mantle and climb
- Physics-driven ragdoll reactions to every obstacle
- Pendulum hammers, rotors, knockback traps and a zero-gravity room
- Rain, wet reflective floors, holographic billboards and a city skyline below you
- End screen with run time, falls and hits: chase your best run
- Main menu, pause menu and credits

CONTROLS
W A S D - Move
Mouse - Camera
Left Shift - Sprint
C - Crouch (while sprinting: slide)
Space - Jump / Vault / Climb
Esc or P - Pause
F11 - Fullscreen
Gamepad supported (Start = pause)

HOW TO PLAY
1. Download and unzip KineticProtocolRun_v1.0_Win64.zip
2. Open the KineticProtocolRun folder and run KineticProtocolRun.exe
3. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click "More info" > "Run anyway" (the game is not digitally signed)

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10/11 64-bit - DirectX 12 compatible GPU - about 1 GB of disk space

ABOUT THE PROJECT
Kinetic Protocol Run is a student project for the Game Design and Unreal Engine 5 courses at ITS Academy ICT Piemonte. It covers the whole level design process: from the greybox blockout to the final polished environment, with lighting, VFX, audio, UI and a packaged build.

CREDITS
Game & Level Design, Environment Art: Lorenzo D'Angelo
ITS Academy ICT Piemonte - 2026

Made with Unreal Engine 5
Character animation & movement: Game Animation Sample Project by Epic Games
City background: "Simple Sci-Fi Futuristic Distance Deco Rooftops" (Fab)
Sound effects & music: Pixabay (Pixabay Content License)
```
